OUR PURPOSE

Making circuit analysis easier and approachable.

Electrical circuits can feel complicated when equations and theory seem disconnected from practical applications. lyZer aims to help bridge that gap by bringing circuit analysis into a more accessible digital experience. Our goal is to support curiosity, strengthen understanding, and encourage users to approach problems with confidence.

THE LYZER APPROACH

Built around understanding.

We believe circuit analysis should be more accessible. It should help you easily understand when a circuit is safe to work with.

01
